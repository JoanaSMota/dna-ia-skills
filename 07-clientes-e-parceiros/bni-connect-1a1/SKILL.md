# BNI Connect — registrar 1a1

## Login
1. Open `https://mybni.com/` and sign in.
2. Open **BNI Connect** from the launcher → dashboard `https://www.bniconnectglobal.com/web/dashboard/`.

## Create / send (modern dashboard — preferred)
1. On the **1-a-1’s** card, click **Enviar** (not only Revisão).
2. If the other member is **outside your chapter**, click **Procurar em outros Grupos**.
3. Search by first + last name (at least 2 characters in one field).
4. Select the correct row with the **row checkbox** (not the name link). Prefer matching chapter name.
5. Confirm **Tem certeza?** with OK, then wait for the form to return with the member label filled.
6. Fill required fields: **Localização**, **Tópicos da conversa**, **Data**. Keep **Convidado por** as the logged-in member when appropriate.
7. Click **Enviar**. If a duplicate warning appears, decide with the user before continuing.

## Important status rule
After a successful member submit, BNI may show: the slip stays **Rascunho** until the chapter **VP sends the next meeting’s PALMS report**. Do not treat **Completo** as proof the Enviar failed; Completo often appears only after PALMS.

## Avoid
- Legacy `editSlips` / `editOneToOneSlip` for cross-chapter members: the underlying `<select>` often stays on the placeholder and returns *Insira o membro com qual se reuniu*.
- Clicking the member **name link** in search (opens profile) instead of the checkbox.
- Assuming the visible name label means the chapter combobox value is set; native Enviar after checkbox+confirm is what works.

## Verify
Open **Relatório de 1 a 1** (Revisão on the 1-a-1 card) and confirm the row (date, member, location, topics, status).
